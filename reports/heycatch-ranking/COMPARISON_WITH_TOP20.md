## What agrees

Home Services maps to Field Services at rank 1; Beauty remains rank 2. The original top four all remain in the later top four. Both reports prioritize one narrow buyer and demonstrable workflow, useful organic content, and explicit launch conditions. Neither certifies production readiness or forecasts revenue. “Paid” in the earlier report means acquiring paying customers organically, not prioritizing paid advertising.

## What conflicts or needs correction

- Grants and Pet Grooming exchanged ranks 3 and 4 without measured demand or activation evidence. Treat that as an unsupported preference change, not a discovery.
- Restaurant moved from 5 to 16, and its offer changed from direct orders to supplier-charge review. The later report did not establish that the original direct-order opportunity became worse.
- Parcel/LTL invoice audit moved from 9 into Logistics at 35. The broad logistics position should not erase the earlier narrow shipping-audit opportunity.
- The SOW generator was assigned to Procurement at 39, although the earlier paying buyer was an agency selling services. Review this classification; it is not a reason to demote the agency SOW offer. No grouping was changed in this comparison.
- CAM reconciliation moved from 10 to 15; marketplace reimbursement moved from 11 to 6; dance studios moved from 17 to Sports & Fitness at 7. These are revised editorial preferences across different groups, not demonstrated changes in acquisition performance.
- The earlier report cites selected original business logic and launch blockers. The later report inspected merged feature definitions, shared local behavior and prior test evidence. Those inspections answer different implementation questions; native record pages do not establish migration of the source workflow.

## Which to use

Keep the earlier TOP20 as the baseline for choosing a narrow paying-customer experiment. Use the 64-platform report as a portfolio map and source of wider growth plans, not as an evidence-backed replacement of that baseline. Preserve the earlier first three: Home Services → Field Services; BeautyHQ → Beauty; Grant Workspace → Nonprofit. Choose the actual executable workflow and verify its launch conditions; do not assume the merged app implements everything in the original.

Re-rank comparable narrow offers under a single rubric after gathering buyer interviews, qualified demo responses, activation/return usage and launch-readiness evidence. Apply HeyCatch's wider research, content/search, conversion, onboarding, email and analytics approach to those offers. A new framework alone is not evidence that the old priorities were wrong.

This reconciliation reads the two reports and current source assignments. It does not rerun original workflows, change rankings/groupings, contact customers or access a HeyCatch account.
